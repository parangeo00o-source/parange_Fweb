Fixed identity artwork: parange-title.svg / parange-subtitle.svg

Reference: the user-supplied NEW ARCHIVE / Brooklyn,New York / ©2018 print.
The original font is unknown. Arial Bold was selected by comparing the capital
A/R/W, lowercase forms, stroke ends and proportions with the reference.

Lettering was outlined from the locally installed Arial Bold for these two
fixed strings only. No font binary is embedded or redistributed.

Title: PARANGE; tracking -0.012 em.
Subtitle: Objects, Graphics & Things.; tracking -0.015 em.
Original glyph kerning retained. A 0.009 em currentColor stroke with round joins
adds a small ink spread to the print-like edges. No raster blur or texture.
Both SVGs inherit the header's ivory color. Corresponding text is preserved
in archive.ts for accessibility, while decorative paths are aria-hidden.
